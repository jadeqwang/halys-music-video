# HALYS — style bible (v1)

The rules every frame obeys. Background and reasoning: TREATMENT.md. Timing: SHOTLIST.md. Look-dev evidence: production/lookdev/.

## Two engines, five worlds

Every plate is analysed once (structure-tensor flow, edges, tone, colour, matte, depth). Two drawing engines read that analysis.

**The brush engine**: curved, tapered strokes laid along the flow field, coarse to fine, with impasto lit from upper-left, canvas weave and varnish.
- **BRONZE** (Act I): Caravaggio tenebrism. Near-black umber world; one designed light pool, warm and fast-falling; rim light only on the lit side, broken.
  Palette (no saturated blue, after the Greek four-colour palette): bone black #0b0806, raw umber #3b2a1a, burnt umber #5a3a22, burnt sienna #8a4a2a,
  yellow ochre #b8862f, Naples yellow #e3c27a, lead white #efe6d2, vermilion #c2361f, madder #7d1f24, muted verdigris #4a6a58.
  The low sun is a flat blazing disk, golden-orange near the horizon, with no rings. The eclipse fraction drives the bite, sharpening shadows, colour drain and metallic light.
- **MARBLE** (breakdown → Thales): **painted statuary**, the same brush engine in stone. Think de Chirico's metaphysical squares and Böcklin's stillness,
  not a 3D render. Cool silvery whites #ece6db / #d9d6cf, grey-violet mids #8a8c90, deep shadow #16161c; long smooth strokes, little impasto, crisp
  silhouettes, blank carved eyes, a few thin veins. Depth-derived light sculpts the form: cool soft key from above (the corona), warm orange rim from the
  360° horizon glow. Sky: desaturated navy-black (#0d1018, never indigo-violet), Jupiter, a soft orange horizon gradient.
- **GOLD** (final chorus, the dive's landing): BRONZE in returning golden-hour sunlight. Same palette pushed warm and high-key, plus gold leaf #d4a840.
  The light pool becomes the whole frame. Glory clouds are painted (Tiepolo/Rubens), never lens-flare rays.

**The line engine**: luminous field lines tracing the same flow field, plus particles.
- **CORONA** (Drop 1): pearl #f3efe6 lines on navy-black #05070c, signal-orange #f08a2a rims and horizon. Hierarchy: subject contours brightest and crispest,
  interior lines spaced by tone, background sparse, lots of black. Massed soldiers are spear ticks with bright tips. The corona is the most beautiful
  thing in frame: bright limb ring, asymmetric streamers, polar plumes, 2–3 tiny prominences, a crisp black disk. Restrained glow, no blanket bloom.
  Kick: line-thickness pulse plus a radial push from the sun. Brass stabs: an optional 2-frame inversion (dark lines on pearl).
- **ORBIT** (Drop 2): CORONA at cosmic scale. **The only saturated blue in the film is Earth** (and the RARE EARTH circle on her back).

**INK** (the room) is neither engine. Clean anime cel: flat fills, one shadow tone, crisp tapered line art, painted flat-shape backgrounds, no
depth-of-field blur, no Ghibli-soft gradients, no yellow cast. Her palette: hair #101114, jacket #f2f0ea, orange #f08a2a, navy #1e2433.

## Transitions follow the material logic

- **Disk wipe.** The Moon's black disk grows to fill the frame and opens on the next world. This is the default world change.
- **Drop 1 (paint → light).** Diamond ring, white, the black pupil, and the world returns as lines.
- **Breakdown (light → stone).** The field lines slow and widen into long white brush strokes.
- **Spark (stone → flesh).** The diamond-ring burst spreads outward; where its light arrives, the marble palette warms into BRONZE/GOLD.
- **The dive (light → paint).** Lines condense into strokes as we land at the Halys.
- **The pull-back (paint → light → ink).** Out through the sky to the line-drawn Earth, into the monitor bezel.
- **The rewind** (cold open): strokes swirl backwards.

## Cadence

Master 60 fps. BRONZE/GOLD/MARBLE draw at 12 (MARBLE may go to 30 for the drifting camera if strokes stay coherent), CORONA/ORBIT at 60, and INK on
twos with real holds. Boil, never strobe: stroke seeds are stable per region, and only detail re-rolls between drawings.

## Camera and cutting

Cut on the beat and hold. No perpetual floaty push-ins and no morph transitions. A camera moves only when the move means something: the descent
into the valley, the CORONA orbit (reality revealed as 3D), the marble drift (frozen time), the dive and the pull-back. Symmetry and centre-lock for
the two armies (equal dignity) and for the ring at the K-pop moments.

## Type

Roles and effects as in SHOTLIST.md and production/type/NOTES.md:

| Role | Use | Look |
|---|---|---|
| CARVED | big lyrics, title | gilded Cinzel lit by the scene's light |
| PLAQUE | facts | small tracked caps |
| CHOP | drops | expanded black Archivo filled with field lines |
| INSCR | verse 2 | Cormorant italic |
| MONO | terminal, HUD | JetBrains Mono |

Spelling is exact. Reading speed ≤ 15 characters/second. Subtitles ≥ 4.5 % of frame height. Everything inside the 4:5 safe zone, or re-flowed per aspect.

## Never

Indigo-violet gradients, Inter, glassmorphism, blanket bloom or chromatic aberration, Perlin swirls, Matrix rain, synthwave grids,
glowing-eyed statues, Eye-of-Providence triangles, lens-flare god rays, orange-teal grading, real brands or cloned product UIs,
Musk iconography, "AGI" on screen, modern military signifiers, anything photoreal enough to clip as footage, villainised Medes.
