# HALYS v2: revision brief

The director's notes on the v1 film (`release/Halys_1080p60_hevc.mp4`, 2026-10-03), what we decided to do about each, and the
rules for the agents doing it. Everything in STYLE_BIBLE.md, TREATMENT.md and RESEARCH.md still holds unless this file says otherwise.

## 1. The notes (verbatim)

> * 0:27, 0:29, 0:41, 0:45, 0:48, the sun looks a little too sharp. like it should still have some brush stroke at the edges to be in the art style.
> * 0:45 - 1v1 combat, I'd expect background characters to also be fighting unless we know of a 1v1 somewhere
> * 1:30 - looking up. They should be mid action shot looking up, not standing in a row.
> * 3:06 - I like most of this but the prediction market is a bit cheesy. I'd rather have the actual methods of calculations Thales would
>   actually have used (geometry based, like most Greek math at that time), even though Jade outside the simulation uses algebra and programming.
> * 4:22 - shrug is a bit silly (even though the whole thing is silly, the warriors are still serious dudes)
> * 4:28 - arm patch off is coming off sleeve; it's fine for the rest of the room to stay in an abstract style, but she should be more anime
>   style herself, and the RARE EARTH on the jacket should bend and flow with the fabric.
> * 4:30 - Jade turning around on the beat is a bit too dramatic, maybe we go back in universe and the kings are shown signing a peace treaty
>   (or whatever hand shaking was customary for that era), and then we cut back to Jade outside the simulation.
> * 4:34 - when we cut back, she should have a mischievious grin (not serious looking), kinda like this reference expression (hat photo)
>   https://github.com/jadeqwang/rewind-music-video/blob/claude/magical-faraday-dbldp4/refs/jade/IMG_20170223_162015.jpg
>
> They're probably not throwing their swords into the river in reality as metal was scarce. They're probably mid action shot, are shocked
> and just drop them.

> * when you're done, let's render a small version before we move onto a full size one.

## 2. Decisions

| # | Shots | v1 | v2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | every painted Sun: S01–S30, S58–S62, S75–S76 (named: S11, S12, S16, S17, S18) | a flat, crisp, vector-clean disk | **The Sun is painted.** The limb is built from brush strokes (dabs that follow and break the edge, varied length and value, impasto), so the disk sits *in* the paint. It still reads as a blazing low Sun, and the Moon's bite in Act I still reads as a real partial eclipse (a clean curved edge, painted, never a blur). |
| 2 | S17 (44.73–46.49), S18 (46.49–51.72) | the duel alone on an empty river; two silhouettes under the Sun | **A battle, not a duel.** The Mede's spear turned by the Lydian's lion shield stays the foreground action, with the two lines fighting in the shallows behind them. S18's silhouettes become a fighting line along the horizon, and nobody notices the bite. |
| 3 | S28 (89.22–93.00) | six warriors standing in a row, looking up | **Frozen mid-fight, looking up.** Pairs locked in combat (a thrust caught on a shield, a raised sword, grappling) stop mid-action and turn their faces up, one after another, cut on the 91.31 boom. `A HALO IN THE SKY` / `WARRIORS AWED` stay on the circle around the Sun. |
| 4 | S53 (183.34–187.65) | gold construction lines + a prediction-market card at 186.4 | **Cut the card. Keep the rest. Show what the ancient sources credit Thales with, as Greek geometry.** The headline is the *1/720*: Diogenes Laertius 1.24 says that, according to some, Thales declared the Sun to be 1/720 of its circle and the Moon the same fraction of its own, so the two disks look the same size and the Moon can exactly cover the Sun. Draw it as compass-and-straightedge construction (circle, 720 divisions, two equal disks closing to totality), with the shadow-stick similar triangles and his triangle-in-a-semicircle. Use lettered points in the Greek manner, gold incised lines, nothing that looks like UI. We do not know how (or whether) he predicted it; Herodotus says only that he foretold the year. So nothing on screen may claim more than the sources do. |
| 5 | S61/S61b (208.70–211.44), S62 (211.44–215.29), S75 (259.36–260.62), S76 (260.62–262.72) | weapons thrown into the river; a sword tossed into the sky; a formation pose; a straggler who shrugs | **Bronze was precious: nobody throws it away.** Caught mid-action, shocked, they let go: swords and spears fall from opening hands onto the bank and into the shallows. S61b: one hand opens, its sword drops into the shallows during the held "blade". S62: a dropped sword stands upright in the mud while its owner walks away, a vertical that match-cuts to the rocket on the pad at 215.29. S75–S76: a battle line caught mid-fight; on **BLADE** every blade falls at once and a late one lands on the 261.05 hit. No shrug, no posing, faces serious. |
| 6 | S78 (266.12–270.04) | Jade drawn in an abstract, near-realistic flat style; the 1420 MHz patch floats off her sleeve; RARE EARTH printed flat | **She is anime; the room stays abstract.** Her hair, face, jacket and line art match the anime character sheet (`Pasted image.png`) and the S80–S81 close-ups. The patch is stitched to the left sleeve: it moves, turns and is occluded with the arm. The blue circle and RARE EARTH on her back follow the folds and shading of the jacket. |
| 7 | S79 (270.04–273.40) | she spins the chair to camera on the final chord | **Back in-universe: the kings make peace.** On the final chord, Alyattes of Lydia and Cyaxares of Media swear the peace the way Herodotus 1.74 says Lydians and Medes did: "sworn compacts as the Greeks do; and in addition they cut the outer skin of their arms and lick each other's blood." Show clasped, freshly cut forearms, solemn, in painted dusk light, with one Caravaggio light pool on the clasp; no gore, no licking. The mediators (Syennesis of Cilicia, Labynetus of Babylon) may stand behind. The war ends in a gesture, not a spin. |
| 8 | S80 (273.40–276.95), S81 (276.95–281.0) | deadpan stare, then the wink | **Cut back to a mischievous grin.** Close-up, anime: the closed-lip, one-corner-up, knowing smirk of the reference photo (eyes engaged and slightly narrowed, head a little tilted). She types blind (keys 273.45, 274.05, enter 274.95), the commit prints behind her, then **the wink on the ting at 276.95** (the eyelid crosses her eye like the Moon crossing the Sun), then the end card. The expression comes from the photo; the face and style come from the character sheet. |
| 9 | the whole film | — | **A small preview first.** Changed shots are re-rendered at 1080p into the master frames, and the whole film is encoded as a small 540p preview for review. The full-size release files are made only after approval. |

These files are shared: `tools/plate_specs.py` (append your entries), `production/PLATES.md` (append your plates), `production/FACTCHECK.md`
(your section) and `production/SHOTLIST.md` (your rows). After editing SHOTLIST.md, regenerate with `python3 tools/shotlist.py`. Re-read a shared
file right before you edit it.

**The machine has 4 cores and 15 GB; six agents share it.**
- Run every render or encode through `tools/slot.sh` (2 slots), e.g. `tools/slot.sh node render.mjs --sheet=...`.
- Run every plate-pipeline step through `tools/slot.sh --pipeline` (1 slot), e.g. `tools/slot.sh --pipeline tools/pipeline.sh --all P48`.
- Always pass `--workers=1`. Never start a long render.

**Never write into `video/out/frames`.** Don't use `--frames`, and don't touch `video/out/frames*`. Verify with `--sheet`, `--stills` and short
`--clip`s into `video/out/review_v2/<agent>/`. The director re-renders the master frames after all tasks land.

**Never commit or push.** The director commits. Do not run `git add`, `commit`, `stash`, `checkout` or `reset`.

**Generation.**
- Read `production/TOOLING.md` §1 and `production/PLATES.md` ("What Seedance 2.5 did with these prompts") before spending.
- Use `tools/plates.py` (specs in `tools/plate_specs.py`). Every paid call is logged to `media/genlog.jsonl` automatically.
- Caps: ACT1 $25, GOLD $30, TREATY $15, ROOM $30, SUN/THALES $0.
- Plates are traceable reference only, never shown: one clear action, readable silhouettes, hard light, no text.
- Use song slices as `audio` refs so timing is exact, then verify where the action lands against `video/data/timing.json`, and retime with plate keys.
- Costumes come from RESEARCH.md and the existing boards (`media/boards/`, REFS keys in `plate_specs.py`); do not invent new looks.

**Verification.**
- Look at your frames at 1080p and in 4:5 (`--size=1080x1350`); the 4:5 teasers exist.
- Check the frames on both sides of every cut you touch.
- Use `node render.mjs --list` to confirm shot times.
- Take the director's notes literally: if the note says "mid action", a frozen pose fails.

**Report** (under 250 words): what changed, the files, the plates and their spend, contact-sheet paths, and anything still weak.
