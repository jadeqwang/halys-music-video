# HALYS — shot list (v1, locked to the beat grid)

All times are song seconds (`video/data/timing.json`). Bar numbers are the audio map's bars (B#). Cuts land on the listed downbeat or event.
The tempo drifts from 136 to 142 BPM, so always use these times, never a constant grid.
Master timeline 60 fps. Draw cadence per world: **BRONZE/GOLD 12** (each drawing held 5 frames), **MARBLE 30**, **CORONA/ORBIT 60**,
**INK** on twos with holds. Text is always rendered by us, so spelling is exact. Layout notes are for 16:9; the 4:5 re-flow keeps the same
anchors (text above/below the subject instead of beside it).

Worlds: **BRONZE** (oil + tenebrism) · **CORONA** (field lines of light) · **MARBLE** (frozen statuary) · **GOLD** (BRONZE in returning sunlight) ·
**ORBIT** (CORONA at cosmic scale, the only blue is Earth) · **INK** (anime cel, the room).
Type: **CARVED** = Cinzel caps, gold leaf, lit by the scene · **PLAQUE** = small tracked caps, museum label · **CHOP** = ultra-heavy expanded grotesk filled
with field lines · **INSCR** = Cormorant Garamond italic, small · **MONO** = JetBrains Mono.
